The boundary is the product

Automation compares. A person decides.

Packet Proof does

  • Extract and normalize evidence
  • Compare every required field
  • Surface confidence and mismatches
  • Preserve hashes and an audit trail

Packet Proof never

  • Auto-releases a packet
  • Signs or amends a document
  • Transmits the shipment
  • Represents a customs decision

Nutrient DWS extracted structured JSON content from each source PDF; Packet Proof reconciled those extracted fields deterministically and reserved the release decision for a human.
